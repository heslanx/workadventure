**Reading.**

1. The Gate 1 limits are met in every window of every valid run, with and without the flags: the largest GPU average is 4.29 ms by day and 7.51 ms at dusk, the largest p99 is 8.21 ms, the largest single GPU frame 8.43 ms.
2. The flags do not lower average GPU time. At dusk and at night every flags-on average is above every flags-off average, by at least 0.5 ms; by day the ranges overlap. Every flags-on p99 and maximum is below every flags-off one in the table: by at least 0.2 ms at p99 and at least 0.4 ms at the maximum. The cause of the higher average was not investigated.
3. Dropped frames. In the 18 windows with the flags on (`default1` to `default3`, `run2` to `run4`) the frame interval maximum is 16.80 ms in all of them. In the 12 windows with the flags off, 3 windows had an interval of 33.3 to 33.5 ms: `baseline1` day, `baseline2` day and `baseline2` night. In `baseline1` day the p99 is 33.40 ms and the average 17.28 ms, about 11 intervals of 33 ms (DERIVED from the average); the other two had one. All three are in the earlier pair, which the session noted was taken at a load average of 7 to 8 (not recorded in the reports) and which also ran without `konclui.fx.rim`; the later flags-off pair (`off1`, `off2`, load about 4 per the same notes) had none. These figures are consistent with the flags removing dropped frames and do not separate the flags from the load of the machine. The commit message of `863e8d384` and `benchmark/README.md` say "two of six windows"; the reports show three.
4. The maximum GPU frame at the new defaults is 7.81 ms. The frame interval cannot fall below the display period, so a maximum of 16.80 ms is the display period plus timer rounding. The mandate's "no single frame above 16.7 ms" is met for GPU time and shows no dropped frame in the interval; no claim is made beyond that.

What these numbers do not include: default zoom only (no whole-map zoom); 999 synthetic walkers joined through the repository, not real sockets or the pusher; no normal-map texture in the scene (every window reported `normalMapLoaded: false`) and so no cost of the props' bevel; `konclui.fx.rim` on in every run but two, and it is off by default in the product; no CPU work time, draw-call count or per-frame heap figure; one GPU, one display period (60 Hz) and a machine whose load changed between runs.

### Reading

1. **Compression is the large gain.** It cuts what is downloaded by about four fifths and cuts the phone's first paint from 33.7 s to 8.0 s and its name field from 35.9 s to 10.2 s. It does not change the decoded bytes or the work the browser does with them.
2. **On localhost compression saves no time.** Desktop FCP was 796 ms before and 944 ms after, with TTFB at 32 ms and 63 ms; the third run is back at 784 ms and 31 ms. One run each, on a loaded machine: no claim of a regression is made, and none of a gain. The third run's FCP and LCP of 784 ms are the splash painted by the HTML.
3. **The splash changes what is painted, not when the app works.** Phone FCP and LCP fall from 8.0 s and 10.1 s to 2.66 s. The name field is visible at 10.1 s in the third run, as in the second.
4. **The layout shift is gone** on a first visit (after) and on a reload (after 2): 0.247 to 0 in both.
5. **Total blocking time after the first paint rose because the first paint moved, not because the work grew.** Two more runs of the last build (`webperf-2026-11-22-after3` and `-after4`, same script, same day) give, for before, after, after 2, after 3 and after 4: long tasks until 6 s after the office loaded 1,513, 1,584, 3,254, 1,597 and 1,660 ms in total, with a longest task of 537, 537, 677, 490 and 493 ms; entering the office took 1,544, 1,749, 3,015, 1,659 and 1,636 ms; TBT after FCP 516, 634, 2,160, 1,147 and 1,210 ms. The third run (after 2) is the outlier, taken while the machine was heavily loaded (not recorded in the report). In the two repeats the total main-thread work is what it was before, and TBT after FCP is about 600 ms higher because FCP is now at 84 to 112 ms (the logo painted by the HTML), ahead of the boot task of about 490 ms that used to run before the first paint and so was not counted. The blocking work itself was not reduced.
6. **Thresholds** (web.dev): LCP good under 2.5 s and poor over 4 s; FCP good under 1.8 s; CLS good under 0.1; TBT good under 200 ms. Desktop FCP is good in every run and LCP is good except the second run (2.58 s). TBT is above 200 ms in every desktop run, and the third phone run's 554 ms is above it too (total blocking time counts only the long tasks that start after FCP, which is why the phone's was 0 while its first paint came after its long task).
